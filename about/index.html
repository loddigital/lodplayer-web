About LOD Player and LOD Digital

LOD Player is an independent, cross-platform IPTV and VOD media player developed and published by LOD Digital in Istanbul, Turkey. Built natively for iOS (iPhone and iPad), Android smartphones, tablets, and Android TV / Google TV, LOD Player focuses on offline VOD playback, Smart TV usability, and strict user privacy.

Official Identity and Verified Distribution Channels

To protect users from counterfeit APKs or unrelated software sharing similar abbreviations, always verify that you are installing the official LOD Player published by LOD Digital. Our only official website is https://lodplayer.com/ and our official Smart TV pairing tool is hosted at https://lodplayer.com/sync/.

Official mobile and television store listings are published under Google Play package ID com.lodplayer (https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lodplayer) and Apple App Store ID 6831201648 (https://apps.apple.com/app/lod-player-iptv-player/id6831201648). Official direct Android APK builds are distributed exclusively via the LOD Digital GitHub Releases channel (github.com/loddigital/lodplayer-web).

What LOD Player Does — and What It Does Not Do

LOD Player is strictly a client-side media playback engine supporting M3U, M3U8, and Xtream Codes API playlists provided by the user. Its core features include secure in-app offline VOD downloading, multi-language audio and subtitle track switching, smart resume history, parental (+18) filtering, 5-device cloud synchronization, and 8-digit phone-to-TV playlist transfer.

LOD Digital does not provide, host, stream, promote, or sell any television channels, movies, VOD libraries, or IPTV subscriptions. LOD Player is an independent media utility with no affiliation to Android emulators, gaming software, or unrelated third-party brands.

Transparency, Privacy, and Support

Users can run LOD Player locally with zero account registration, or opt into cloud synchronization to mirror playlists, favorites, and watch progress across 5 screens. Full details on data handling, terms, and self-service deletion are published openly in our Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Account Deletion, and Cloud Data Deletion pages.

For technical assistance, store compliance inquiries, or bug reports, contact LOD Digital directly at support@lodplayer.com.

Supported Devices, Formats, and Language Availability

LOD Player runs natively across iOS (iPhone and iPad), Android smartphones, Android tablets, Android TV, Google TV, and Android-based Smart TV boxes. The application supports Xtream Codes API credentials as well as direct M3U and M3U8 playlist links, with automatic phone-to-TV pairing via an 8-digit code at lodplayer.com/sync.

The official LOD Player website and sync utility support 29 languages worldwide, and LOD Digital continuously updates playback performance, subtitle customization, and D-Pad remote navigation based on community feedback.
