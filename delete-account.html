Account Deletion

Google Play User Data Compliance

This page outlines how to permanently delete your LOD Player account, authentication credentials, and all synchronized cloud data.

Only want to clear your playlists and history without closing your account? Click here for Cloud Data Deletion.

1 Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can immediately and permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data directly from within the LOD Player mobile or Android TV app:

  1. 1
    Open the LOD Player app on your device (Mobile or Android TV).
  2. 2
    Navigate to Settings and select Cloud Synchronization & Devices (Bulut Senkronizasyonu ve Cihazlar).
  3. 3
    Scroll down to the very bottom of the screen.
  4. 4
    Tap or click the Delete Account (Hesabı Sil) button.
  5. 5
    Enter your current account password to authenticate and confirm the deletion request.
  6. ✓
    Upon confirmation, your cloud sync data is immediately erased, your Firebase credentials and account profile are permanently deleted, and you will be signed out.

2 Method 2: Deletion Request via Email

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you may request permanent deletion of your account and cloud data by emailing our support team:

To: support@lodplayer.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request
Requirement: Please send the email from the address associated with your LOD Player account so we can verify account ownership before processing the deletion.

Email requests are processed manually within thirty (30) days of receipt and verification.

Data Retention & Deletion Scope

Detailed overview of data categories processed upon an account deletion request.

Permanently Deleted Data

Erased immediately upon confirmation
  • ✓ Email address, login credentials, and Firebase Auth UID.
  • ✓ All stored IPTV playlist URLs, usernames, passwords, and server credentials.
  • ✓ Watch history, playback resume markers, and favorite channels or movies.
  • ✓ Custom channel sort orders, hidden categories, and playlist preferences.
  • ✓ Linked device identifiers, TV pairing tokens, and active session slots.

Data Retained by Platform Stores

Statutory and app store compliance
  • • Financial transactions, subscription invoices, and payment histories are maintained strictly by the Google Play Store or Apple App Store pursuant to statutory tax and commercial recordkeeping obligations.
  • • LOD Digital retains no credit card numbers, payment instruments, or database backups once account deletion completes.
