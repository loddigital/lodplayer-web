Cloud Data Deletion

Google Play User Data Compliance

This page outlines how to clear and permanently delete all your synchronized cloud data (playlists, watch history, settings) while keeping your LOD Player account active.

Want to permanently close and delete your entire account? Click here for Account Deletion.

1 Method 1: Instant In-App Data Deletion (Recommended)

You can immediately wipe all your synchronized cloud playlists, watch history, and server credentials directly within the app in two simple steps:

  1. 1
    Open the LOD Player app on your device, go to Settings, and select Cloud Synchronization & Devices (Bulut Senkronizasyonu ve Cihazlar).
  2. 2
    Scroll down to the very bottom of the screen and select Delete Cloud Data (Bulut Verimi Sil).
Note: When you select "Delete Cloud Data", all your cloud playlists, watch history, and sync settings are immediately erased from the server. Your account remains active so you can continue using LOD Player and set up new playlists whenever you wish.

2 Method 2: Deletion Request via Email

If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your device, you may request deletion of your cloud sync data by emailing our support team:

To: support@lodplayer.com
Subject: Cloud Data Deletion Request
Requirement: Please send the email from the address registered with your LOD Player account, stating clearly that you request deletion of your cloud sync data.

Email requests are processed manually within thirty (30) days of receipt and verification.

Data Retention & Deletion Scope

Detailed overview of data categories processed upon a cloud data deletion request.

Permanently Deleted Cloud Data

Erased immediately upon confirmation
  • ✓ All stored IPTV playlist URLs, usernames, passwords, and server credentials.
  • ✓ Cloud watch history, playback resume markers, and favorite channels or movies.
  • ✓ Custom channel sort orders, hidden categories, and cloud playlist settings.
  • ✓ Cloud synchronization tokens and TV pairing codes.

Retained Data

Account & app store compliance
  • • User Account: Your LOD Player user login (email and authentication record) remains active so you can continue using the application without re-registering.
  • • Store Purchases: Financial transactions, invoices, and subscription receipts are maintained exclusively by Google Play or Apple App Store pursuant to statutory tax and commercial obligations.
  • • Payment Security: LOD Digital holds no credit card numbers or payment details on its servers.
