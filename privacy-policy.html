2. Local-Only Use (Without an Account)

You are not required to create an account to use the core media playback features of LOD Player. When using LOD Player without logging into a cloud account:

  • Playlists & Media Credentials: Your playlist URLs, Xtream API usernames, passwords, and custom playlist configurations reside exclusively in local encrypted storage on your device.
  • Playback History & Preferences: Your watch history, resume timestamps, favorites, subtitle/audio selections, channel sorting, and hidden channels remain strictly on your physical device.
  • Offline Downloads: Downloaded VOD media files are stored locally in your device's protected internal app storage and are accessible only through the LOD Player app.

5. Security & Encryption

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app, web portal, and Firebase servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Strict RTDB Security Rules: Realtime Database paths are locked down with server-enforced rules. Only authenticated users with verified email addresses can access or modify their personal synchronization tree.
  • Local Storage Encryption: On-device data is stored using high-performance encrypted MMKV storage with AES encryption.
  • Five-Device Enforcement: Accounts are hardware-restricted to a maximum of 5 concurrent active device slots to prevent credential sharing and abuse.
Important Security Notice: While we enforce robust security controls, LOD Player does not offer end-to-end encryption (E2EE) for stored playlist credentials. Because no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% impenetrable, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Users are encouraged to maintain strong, unique account passwords.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA-CPRA, KVKK)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess specific legal rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may update or correct your email address and profile credentials at any time.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated cloud data permanently.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You may object to data processing carried out under our legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent (such as personalized advertising), you can withdraw consent at any time without penalty.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to file a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority (e.g., KVKK in Turkey, the ICO in the UK, or relevant EU Data Protection Authorities).

How to exercise your rights: You can delete your account instantly within the mobile/TV app by navigating to Settings > Account > Delete Account, visit our dedicated Account Deletion Page, or email our privacy team at support@lodplayer.com. We respond to all formal data subject requests within 30 days.

8. Advertising & Consent Management

We use ironSource LevelPlay to display advertisements in our free version. LevelPlay and its advertising partners (including ironSource, Unity Ads, and Google) may use device identifiers, cookies, and IP addresses to serve and measure advertisements.

  • Personalized Ads & CMP: In regions where required (such as the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), we deploy a certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) to collect your explicit consent before collecting your device's Advertising ID. On iOS devices, we adhere strictly to Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework.
  • Opting Out: You can manage or reset your advertising identifier and opt out of personalized tracking at any time through your device system settings (e.g., "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Delete advertising ID" on Android, or "Allow Apps to Request to Track" on iOS). Upgrading to LOD Player Premium completely removes all advertisements.

9. Device Permissions

LOD Player requests specific permissions only when essential to deliver features locally:

  • Storage & Foreground Service: Required for offline VOD downloading and media caching on mobile devices. Downloads operate while the app is active or executing through foreground notifications.
  • Microphone (Speech-to-Text): Requested strictly to provide the voice search function. Speech recognition is processed completely offline and locally on your device; we never record or transmit your voice data to remote servers.

10. Children's Privacy

LOD Player is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Children under these ages are prohibited from creating a cloud account. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at support@lodplayer.com, and we will promptly delete the data.

11. Web Sync Portal Privacy

When using our web synchronization portal (lodplayer.com/sync), no tracking cookies or persistent third-party analytics are stored on your browser. Temporary authentication state is maintained strictly within your current browser tab's sessionStorage. The playlist details you input are transmitted exclusively to the designated TV pairing room and are removed after pairing.
