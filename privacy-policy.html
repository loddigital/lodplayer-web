Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026

This policy is provided in English; translations, if any, are for convenience only.

Thank you for choosing LOD Player. This Privacy Policy explains transparently how LOD Player collects, uses, stores, synchronizes, and safeguards your information across iOS (iPhone and iPad), Android, Android TV, and our web synchronization portal.

1. Data Controller & Contact

The data controller responsible for the processing of your personal data under this Privacy Policy is:

Data Controller: LOD Digital (Developer Representative), Istanbul, Turkey

Contact & Privacy Inquiries: support@lodplayer.com

2. Local-Only Use (Without an Account)

You are not required to create an account to use the core media playback features of LOD Player. When using LOD Player without logging into a cloud account:

  • Playlists & Media Credentials: Your playlist URLs, Xtream API usernames, passwords, and custom playlist configurations reside exclusively in local encrypted storage on your device.
  • Playback History & Preferences: Your watch history, resume timestamps, favorites, subtitle/audio selections, channel sorting, and hidden channels remain strictly on your physical device.
  • Offline Downloads: Downloaded VOD media files are stored locally in your device's protected internal app storage and are accessible only through the LOD Player app.

3. Optional Cloud Synchronization (With an Account)

If you choose to register an account, LOD Player provides an optional cloud sync service powered by Google Firebase Realtime Database (hosted in the EU / Belgium). This enables you to synchronize your IPTV playlists, playback progress, and preferences across up to 5 authorized personal devices.

6. Security & Encryption

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app, web portal, and Firebase servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Strict RTDB Security Rules: Realtime Database paths are locked down with server-enforced rules. Only authenticated users with verified email addresses can access or modify their personal synchronization tree.
  • Local Storage Encryption: On both iOS and Android, sensitive on-device data (such as playlist credentials, playback history, and session tokens) is stored inside the application's protected sandbox container using MMKV storage with AES encryption. General temporary playlist caches remain strictly isolated inside the protected app container.
  • Five-Device Enforcement: Accounts are hardware-restricted to a maximum of 5 concurrent active device slots to prevent credential sharing and abuse.
Important Security Notice: While we enforce robust security controls, LOD Player does not offer end-to-end encryption (E2EE) for stored playlist credentials. Because no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% impenetrable, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Users are encouraged to maintain strong, unique account passwords.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA-CPRA, KVKK)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess specific legal rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may update or correct your email address and profile credentials at any time.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You have the absolute right to delete your account and all associated cloud data permanently.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You may object to data processing carried out under our legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent (such as personalized advertising or iOS tracking), you can withdraw consent at any time without penalty.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to file a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority (e.g., KVKK in Turkey, the ICO in the UK, or relevant EU Data Protection Authorities).

How to exercise your rights: You can delete your account or cloud data instantly within the iOS (iPhone/iPad), Android, or Android TV app by navigating to Settings > Cloud Synchronization & Devices > Delete Account (or Delete Cloud Data), visit our dedicated Account Deletion Page or Cloud Data Deletion Page, or email our privacy team at support@lodplayer.com. We respond to all formal data subject requests within 30 days.

Active Subscriptions Notice: Deleting your LOD Player account permanently deletes your login credentials and cloud data, but it does not automatically cancel an active Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Please manage or cancel active subscriptions directly in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.

8. Third-Party Services & International Transfers

We engage trusted third-party service providers to facilitate infrastructure, cross-platform subscription billing, stability monitoring, and free-tier advertising:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Cloud database infrastructure (hosted in europe-west1 / Belgium), user authentication, and Firebase Crashlytics crash reporting (Firebase Privacy).
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: Cross-platform in-app purchase management and receipt validation for Monthly/Yearly Premium subscriptions across Apple App Store and Google Play (RevenueCat Privacy Policy).
  • Advertising Partners (Free Ad-Supported Version Only): On iOS (iPhone and iPad), mediation and ads are served exclusively via Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and Unity Ads (Unity & ironSource Privacy Policy). On Android, advertising partners include Unity LevelPlay / ironSource, Unity Ads, and Google Mobile Ads (Google Privacy Policy).
  • Platform App Stores: Apple App Store (iPhone and iPad distribution and payment processing) and Google Play Store (Android and Android TV distribution and payment processing).

Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Turkey (such as to servers in the United States), transfers are executed under approved legal transfer mechanisms, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF).

9. Advertising, ATT & Consent Management

We use Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation to display advertisements in our free version. LevelPlay and its active advertising partners (ironSource and Unity Ads on iOS; ironSource, Unity Ads, and Google Mobile Ads on Android) may process device signals, IP addresses, and—where permitted—advertising identifiers to serve and measure advertisements.

  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS devices (iPhone and iPad), free-tier users are presented with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt at launch asking whether to allow tracking ("Allow" or "Ask App Not to Track"). If you select "Ask App Not to Track" (or disable tracking in iOS Settings), LOD Player does not access your device's IDFA for cross-app tracking, propagates your opt-out signal to LevelPlay (consent and CCPA flags), and serves only non-personalized / contextual ads (alongside privacy-preserving Apple SKAdNetwork attribution).
  • Android Regional Consent (CMP): On Android devices in regions where required (such as the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), we deploy Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) Consent Management Platform to collect your explicit consent before serving personalized advertisements, and you can review or update your consent choices in App Settings at any time.
  • Opting Out & Premium Ad-Free Access: You can manage or reset your advertising identifier and opt out of personalized tracking at any time through your device system settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on iOS, or Opt out of Ads Personalization / Delete advertising ID on Android). Upgrading to LOD Player Premium removes all advertisements and disables ad tracking prompts.

10. Device Permissions

LOD Player requests specific system permissions only when needed to deliver core features:

  • Local Storage & Download Notifications: Downloaded VOD media files are saved strictly inside the application's protected sandbox storage. On iOS, LOD Player requests local notification permission at launch solely to display offline VOD download progress and completion alerts (no remote push notifications are sent). On Android, downloads execute via a foreground service notification. Photo library permission declarations on iOS apply only if local media selection or saving is invoked.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition (Voice Search): Requested strictly when you use the voice search feature. On iOS, speech recognition uses Apple's native SFSpeechRecognizer framework, under which audio may be processed by Apple's speech recognition servers to convert your speech into text according to Apple's privacy terms. On Android, recognition uses the platform speech service. LOD Digital never records, stores, or transmits your voice audio to LOD Digital servers.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS Free Tier Only): Non-premium iOS users are shown Apple's ATT prompt at app launch to choose whether personalized ad tracking is allowed, as described in Section 9.

11. Children's Privacy

LOD Player is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Children under these ages are prohibited from creating a cloud account. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at support@lodplayer.com, and we will promptly delete the data.

12. Web Sync Portal Privacy

When using our web synchronization portal (lodplayer.com/sync), no tracking cookies or persistent third-party analytics are stored on your browser. Temporary authentication state is maintained strictly within your current browser tab's sessionStorage. The playlist details you input are transmitted exclusively to the designated TV pairing room and are removed after pairing.

LOD Player is strictly an independent media player software application. We do not provide, host, distribute, broadcast, or sell any playlists, live television channels, movies, or video content of any kind. Users must supply their own legally obtained streams and credentials.

Any playlist information you save to your cloud account is processed solely to provide synchronization services across your authorized devices. LOD Digital holds no affiliation with third-party IPTV providers and disclaims all liability for the nature or legality of content streamed by users.

14. Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. In the event of material modifications, we will notify you through an in-app notice or prominent banner. Continued use of LOD Player following notification signifies acceptance of the updated terms.
